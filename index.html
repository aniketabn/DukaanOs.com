Payments

            
How will customers pay?

            
Pick the form factors you actually take at the counter. Only these appear in payment recon, a static QR payment needs your nod to tag, while a card machine settles itself.

            

              

                
Soundbox · audio QRstatic counter QR with voice alerts · rental device · payments land decoupled, tagged in recon

                
              

              

                
Static QR standeeprinted counter QR, customer enters the amount · payments land decoupled, tagged in recon

                
              

              

                
Dynamic QR (DQR)amount-encoded QR generated per sale in the app, payments auto-match the bill, no manual recon

                
              

              

                
Card machine (EDC)debit · credit · RuPay POS, contactless under ₹5,000 needs no PIN · auto-matched on settlement

                
              

              

                
Link PayUPI payment links shared on WhatsApp / SMS, amount is fixed in the link · payments auto-match, no manual recon

                
              

              

                
All-in-One deviceevery form factor in one device, sound box, static QR, card swipe & dynamic QR screen · QR payments announced, DQR & card auto-match
